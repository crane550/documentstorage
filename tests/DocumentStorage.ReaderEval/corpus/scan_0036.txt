Volunteer Safety Team
TRAINING HANDOUT - Session 2: Greeting and observing

Learning objectives
By the end of this session you will be able to:
  - describe the three posts at the main entrance
  - recognise when to call the team lead
  - complete the incident note form

Key points
  1. Arrive 20 minutes before the first service.
  2. Work in pairs. Never leave a post unattended.
  3. Radios stay on channel 2.

Discussion questions
  What would you do if a visitor refuses to give a name?

Practice exercise: role-play at the side door (10 minutes).
Next session: first aid basics.
