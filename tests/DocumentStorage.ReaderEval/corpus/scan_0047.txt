Grandma's Skillet Cornbread

Makes one 10 inch skillet        Bake 425 F, 20 to 25 minutes

Ingredients
  1 cup yellow cornmeal
  1 cup flour
  1 tablespoon baking powder
  1/2 teaspoon salt
  1 cup milk
  1 egg
  1/4 cup melted butter, plus more for the skillet

Directions
  Heat the buttered skillet in the oven. Stir the dry ingredients
  together, then add the milk, egg and butter. Pour into the hot
  skillet and bake until golden.
